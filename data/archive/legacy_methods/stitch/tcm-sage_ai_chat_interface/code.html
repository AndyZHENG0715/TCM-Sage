Liver Qi Stagnation & Insomnia

Today, 9:41 AM

Explain the relationship between Liver Qi Stagnation and insomnia, specifically focusing on the Hun (Ethereal Soul) disturbance.

auto_awesome

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, the Liver is responsible for the smooth flow of Qi and stores the Blood. The Hun (魂), or Ethereal Soul, is housed within the Liver Blood.

When Liver Qi stagnates, it can turn into Fire or simply fail to nourish the Blood adequately. If the Liver Blood is deficient or agitated by Heat, the Hun loses its residence and wanders at night, leading to insomnia characterized by dream-disturbed sleep, difficulty falling asleep, or waking at specific times (1-3 AM) .

Classical texts describe this as "The Liver stores the Blood, and the Blood houses the Soul. When the Liver is affected by evils, the Soul is unsettled" .

Section: The Four Qi and Tuning the Spirit

The three months of spring are called "wasting and unfolding". Heaven and earth are born together; the ten thousand things typically flourish. Night bed, early rising, broad strides in the courtyard...

"The Liver stores the blood and the blood houses the soul (Hun). When the liver is affected by pathogenic heat or constrained qi, the soul is unsettled and wanders, causing wakefulness."

To generate without killing, to give without taking, to reward without punishing. This is the correspondence to the qi of spring and the way to nourish life. Contrary to this harms the liver...
